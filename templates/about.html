About this project

Object Outline Detector was made for the Computer Vision assignment under VITyarthi, at VIT Bhopal, in my 3rd year.

You upload an image and type the object you want to find. The app locates it with YOLO11 segmentation, then traces its outline and analyses its shape with classical computer-vision methods. If you leave the object blank, it falls back to a structural outline of the whole image.

  • YOLO11 segmentation
  • Canny edges
  • Hough lines
  • Harris corners
  • Hessian blobs
  • Flask
